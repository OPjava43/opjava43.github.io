Note: The BlueWater non-traumatic hemorrhagic stroke care quality bundle has the following components for care of patients with ischemic stroke:
1. Door to CT in 25min (15min for MCH) or less if w/in 3.5hrs (210min) LKW
2. Timely anticoagulation reversal agent w/in 60min* of CT read, if applicable
3. BP control medication started w/in 60min of CT read if hypertensive OR from time of noted hypertension if not hypertensive before CT read time (Target for SAH if SBP ≥140 or DBP of ≥90, and 2) ICH if SBP ≥160 or DBP ≥100 )
4. No SBP<120 during first 6 hours post antihypertensive treatment (effective 1/1/27)
